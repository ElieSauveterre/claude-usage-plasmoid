# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KDE Plasma users who use Claude Code through a Claude Pro or Max subscription and want to glance at remaining usage without opening another application.

## Product Purpose

Expose the current Claude 5-hour and weekly allowances and their reset times in a compact Plasma-native widget. Success means the panel indicator is immediately legible, the expanded view contains no redundant information, and the age of the data is always visible.

## Brand Personality

Native, concise, dependable. The widget should feel like part of KDE Plasma while carrying a restrained Claude identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like density, decorative gradients, oversized branding, duplicated metrics, and custom controls that behave differently from adjacent Plasma widgets.

## Design Principles

1. Reuse native KDE components before introducing custom drawing.
2. Make the panel glance readable at its real rendered size.
3. Show each fact once and give operational information priority.
4. Keep Claude branding to a single purposeful accent: Claude orange `#d97757`.
5. Preserve clear loading, unavailable, and stale-data states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast at small panel sizes, do not rely on color alone for state, respect the Plasma system font and scaling, and keep all controls keyboard-accessible.
